# Smart Auto Checkpoint

A super fast, intelligent, and zero-spam practice mode checkpoint mod for **Geometry Dash 2.208+**, developed by **SoyRiper**.

## Features

- 🎯 **Smart Key-Event Checkpoints**:
  - **Ground Landing**: Places a checkpoint only after landing on a solid platform from a real fall/jump (requires 0.35s in air, eliminating annoying floor walking spam).
  - **Orbs & Jump Pads**: Instant checkpoint placement on activating any orb, jump pad, or dash ring.
  - **Portals & Gravity**: Instant checkpoint placement when crossing vehicle portals (Ship, Ball, UFO, Wave, Robot, Spider, Swing, Cube) or flipping gravity.
- 🛑 **Pausa al Borrar (X Key Protection)**:
  - Pressing the **`X` key** or the delete checkpoint button pauses auto-checkpointing for **2.0 seconds**, allowing you to delete unwanted checkpoints effortlessly without new ones spawning while you walk or fall.
- ⏱️ **Minimum Cooldown**:
  - Mandatory minimum 0.60s separation between checkpoints to keep your practice screen clean and clutter-free.
